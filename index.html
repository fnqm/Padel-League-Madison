HOW IT WORKS
Season Format
  • •Sign up each week — matchups aren't fixed in advance. I'll pair you with different players for the first 6 weeks.
  • •Signing up doesn't guarantee a spot. It depends on sign-up order and total players that week.
  • •If more than 8 sign up (say 12), the last 4 to sign up will be asked about the 7:00–8:30 slot.
  • •Each day: alternating partners, 8 games per partner combination (2 serves each), 24 games total.
  • •Ranking is based on percentage of games won out of games played.
  • •The final 2 weeks are a playoff round between the top-ranked players.
